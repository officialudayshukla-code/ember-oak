01 · The room

A converted blacksmith's shop, still warm.

The hearth was lit in 2016 and has not gone out since. Chef-owner Amara Vance cooks over it directly — no gas line, no induction, just oak, embers, and a cast-iron line that runs the length of the room.

Seating is thirty-four, deliberately. It means the kitchen can hear the pass, and we can hear you.

04 · Visit

The cellar stairs are the whole way down.

Ember & Oak sits below street level in the former blacksmith's shop on Fenner Street. The room is reached down a flight of fourteen steps from the street door — step-free access is through the kitchen, so tell us in advance and we will meet you there.
